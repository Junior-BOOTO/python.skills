# From evidence to a teaching decision: Grade 3 speaking

**Scenario:** Fictional grade 3 learners discuss household chores in A1 French. The four skills are recorded separately. A group mean is an invitation to examine individual tasks, not a diagnosis of a child.

1. **Check the evidence.** Review oral exchanges for responses to « Tu fais quoi ? » and intelligibility, plus the support provided. Compare the speaking criterion observations from T1 with listening, reading and writing; do not infer that reading strength automatically transfers to speaking.
2. **Choose a goal.** « Je peux demander à un camarade quelle tâche il fait et répondre avec une phrase simple. » Observe a question, a relevant answer and comprehensible familiar words.
3. **Opening (5 minutes).** Display three chore pictures and ask pupils to recognize familiar verbs and explain which chores they already know.
4. **Main task (20 minutes).** **Les élèves vont** listen to a short modeled exchange, select a picture card, and practise in **binômes**. The teacher models one question and one answer, then listens to each pair. A worksheet contains picture prompts and a word bank; a dictionary is used to check an unfamiliar verb; drafts and feedback go into portfolios. Emerging speakers receive sentence frames (« Tu fais… ? » / « Oui, je fais… »); more confident speakers add a frequency word and a reason. Rotate partners for a second attempt.
5. **Closure (5 minutes).** With a new card, each learner asks and answers once. Record separately whether the question is formed, the answer is relevant, and the message is intelligible. Learners select « Je peux poser une question » and « Je peux répondre » only when they demonstrate each action.
6. **Reassess.** Use a fresh but comparable paired prompt, the same three PO criteria and recorded support. Review the work before interpreting any score change.

This is a designed example, not a report of an actual class or measured impact. It corresponds to [Grade 3 Unit 2](https://github.com/Junior-BOOTO/portfolio-fle/blob/main/ib-pyp-french-programme/curriculum/units.md).

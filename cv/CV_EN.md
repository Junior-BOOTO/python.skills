## Selected Personal Projects

Learning projects developed with documented AI assistance. Results below are drawn from repository reports; these projects are separate from paid employment in data science.

### AfriFlow Explain | Transfer Paths and Anomaly Detection

Analysed 11,775 synthetic transfers across 1,800 cases; extracted cycles, temporal chains and transaction-linked patterns, with an interactive interface.

Compared two Isolation Forest models at a fixed budget of 36 alerts on 360 test cases: detected 19 injected scenarios versus 3 without path features, with 17 false positives. Synthetic prototype; no live bank connection.

[GitHub project: afriflow-explain](https://github.com/Junior-BOOTO/python.skills/tree/main/afriflow-explain)

### BikeDemand | Hourly Bike Rental Forecasting

Prepared 17,379 UCI observations; compared Ridge regression and boosting using chronological splits. Used calendar features available one day ahead, avoiding leakage from future weather observations.

Test MAE of 72.35 rentals/hour versus a baseline of 79.79, a 9.3% reduction. Exported and reloaded the model; historical data from 2011–2012.

[GitHub project: bike-demand](https://github.com/Junior-BOOTO/python.skills/tree/main/bike-demand)

### Credit Default | Binary Classification

Applied logistic regression to 30,000 UCI records; split by customer profiles, fitted preprocessing on training data and selected the decision threshold on validation data.

Test ROC-AUC of 0.745 and Average Precision of 0.501; analysed false positives and probability calibration. Historical Taiwan data from 2005; no individual lending decisions.

[GitHub project: credit-default-classification](https://github.com/Junior-BOOTO/python.skills/tree/main/credit-default-classification)

### AdventureWorks | SQL and Data Preparation

Imported nine Microsoft sample business tables into SQLite: 31,465 orders and 121,317 order lines. Used joins, views, CTEs and window functions.

Executed 14 analyses and 3 additional queries; checked data granularity, keys and reconciled totals. Fictional sample database with a documented SQLite adaptation.

[GitHub project: adventureworks-sql](https://github.com/Junior-BOOTO/python.skills/tree/main/adventureworks-sql)
